When ads and trackers completely destroy the experience
September 28, 2026 · 1 min · Javier De Carli

The web became a pretty hostile place to browse/navigate. It’s almost mandatory to have an ad-blocker to stop intrusive ads, popups, trackers and other maliciuos content. And let’s not even talk about mobile because the situation there is even worse.

I think this is making a lot of people to go back to more simple ways to get information. Of course, I don’t think RSS will become popular but who knows. Anyways, I just added RSS support in case you want.
